Every buyer has a budget. The honest question is not "how do I spend as little as possible?" but "where can I cut without buying a problem I will pay for later?"

A 120 kW (roughly 150 kVA) silent set is the size where this trade-off becomes real. It is the entry point for a small factory, a commercial building, or a hotel that cannot afford a breakdown, but it is also the size where the cheapest quote looks tempting.

## What a 120kW set is for

Before we talk about budget, be clear on the fit:

- A small manufacturing unit with a few machines and office load
- A 30- to 50-room hotel with lighting, lifts, and water pumps
- A mid-size supermarket with refrigeration and checkout systems
- A school or training center with lighting and IT room
- A construction compound with tower crane, lighting, and site office

The running load here is usually 80 to 100 kW. The set has headroom for motor starts, and it is not so large that it lugs on partial load.

## Where the savings are safe

If the budget is tight, there are places where you can spend less without regret:

- **Canopy finish.** A powder-coated canopy in a standard color costs less than a painted one. It protects the engine the same.
- **Alternator brand.** A locally reputable alternator is fine for standby duty. You only need a known import alternator if the load is highly sensitive.
- **Controller features.** You do not need a full touchscreen monitoring system with remote GSM if the set is checked weekly on site.
- **Day tank size.** A modest 8-hour tank is fine for standby duty; you only need a 24-hour basement tank for mission-critical sites.

A Shangchai-based set in this class often lands here, because the engine itself is well proven in the Chinese domestic market and widely supported across Asia. The price step versus an imported engine is real, and the machine still does the job.

## Where the savings are dangerous

There are lines you should not cross, even when the salesperson is friendly:

- **Cutting the automatic transfer switch.** A manual start is fine on a farm where someone is on site. On a hotel or a clinic, the set must start itself within seconds.
- **Skipping the sound canopy.** A 120 kW open set is loud. You will end up building a wall around it, which costs more than the canopy would have.
- **Undersizing by one tier.** A 100 kW set for a 90 kW load will spend its life at the edge. The engine overheats, voltage sags, and the warranty voids itself through abuse.
- **No local service.** This is the one that bites. A cheap set with no local workshop is not a purchase, it is a bet.

## The arithmetic of a tight budget

Suppose your budget gives you two options:

1. A 120 kW imported-engine set, fully featured, but no room for a day tank.
2. A 120 kW Shangchai-based set, silent canopy, automatic transfer switch, and an 8-hour tank.

For most small commercial sites, the second option is the wiser buy. You are not paying less for less — you are paying less for the features you actually use, and skipping the badges you do not need.
